# The face — 2026-10-18

⛔ **The observation is the spine of this file. The program's numbers are quoted inside it as evidence, not around it.** A drawn face is exactly the case this rule was written for: the code can say a shape was computed, and only an eye can say what appeared on the glass.

**What was counted.** One unit, one day, one pair of eyes, and one photograph. **What was not counted:** whether the face is beautiful, whether it matches the vendor's firmware frame for frame, whether the smoothing is enough at arm's length, and whether anything is alive behind it.

## What the photograph showed

A photograph of the screen was taken from the front and measured on the host — one column at a time, in the photo's own pixels, then divided by the panel's own row height. ⛔ **No homography was trusted for the row numbers**: the panel's top edge is behind the bezel and its black is not black in a photograph, so the absolute rows cannot be read off a picture. What can be read is the **distance between a shape and its copy**, taken in the same column, and that needs no reference.

| what | the solid part | the second part | the gap between them |
|---|---|---|---|
| left eye | photo rows 2205–2302, about 13 panel rows | 2315–2325, about 1.5 rows | about **1.8 rows** |
| mouth | photo rows 2523–2571, about 7 panel rows | 2604–2616, about 1.7 rows | about **4.4 rows** |

⚠️ **The panel's own row height was 7.33–7.35 photo pixels, taken at five columns across the screen** — that part *is* a measurement, because it uses the panel's two edges and needs no absolute origin.

⇒ **Every shape on the screen had a second, thinner copy of itself below it**, and the distance to the copy **grew down the frame**: about two rows at the eye, about four and a half at the mouth. ⛔ **That growth is the signature of a race, not of a coordinate.** A drawing error would move the whole face by one constant.

## What was wrong

`display.cpp` pushes the frame in chunks of eight rows through one buffer, `g_line[320 * 8]`. `esp_lcd_panel_io_tx_color()` **queues a DMA transfer and returns**; the header it is declared in says exactly what that costs, and the sentence was in the repository's own include path the whole time:

> The caller should take care of the lifecycle of the `color` buffer. Recycling of color buffer should be done in the callback `on_color_trans_done()`.

⛔ **The buffer was refilled immediately with the next eight rows**, so the hardware read rows that had already been overwritten. With `trans_queue_depth = 10`, several chunks could reference the same memory at once. The first chunks were shipped before the overwrite and looked right; the later ones carried their neighbour's rows — **which is why the error grew down the screen and why the top of the face was the part that looked two rows out.**

⚠️ **It was reported four times before it was found, and each report was right about what was seen**: the staircase (it was there — the circles were drawn at one bit), the shift (there too — a chunk boundary through every contour), the two rows (there — at the eye). **A single cause was wearing three faces, and only the photograph showed more than one of them at once.**

## What was changed

- `main/display.cpp` registers `on_color_trans_done` on the panel IO and takes a binary semaphore after every chunk. **One buffer, one transfer at a time.** A whole frame costs about 30 ms; a face changes on an event.
- The timeout is 200 ms per chunk. Exceeded, `Push()` returns false — it does not spin.
- ⛔ **A `-2` row shift was written into `face_bitmap.cpp` on the strength of statement 7, and then removed.** The comment where it stood records why: the vendor's own numbers were already right, and the two rows belonged to the wire. **The file now draws the vendor's rows and says so.**

## What this file does not claim

- **That the face matches the vendor's firmware on the glass.** It matches the vendor's *numbers* — both eye centres, the eyelid's integer offset, the mouth bar, the six emotion pairs — and `esp_lcd_ili9341`'s own `draw_bitmap` was read to confirm the panel path (no gap, `MADCTL 0x08`, no mirror, no swap). ⛔ **The driver's source was not on this host**: the copy read was `espressif/esp-bsp` master, and the vendor pins `==1.2.0`.
- **That a green `tests/test_stackchan_face.py` says anything about the panel.** It never sees SPI.
- **Anything about the goal.** A face appeared, was found wrong, was measured, was mended, and was accepted. **Whether it is alive is not in this file and is not in this repository.**
