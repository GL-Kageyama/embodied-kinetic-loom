# The neck, moved until the author said it was good — 2026-10-18

⛔ **The observation is the spine of this file. The program's numbers are quoted inside it as evidence, not around it.** A curve with no eye behind it is a guess in a measurement's clothes.

**What was counted.** One unit, one pair of eyes, one afternoon, seven segments of postures measured on this unit. **What was not counted:** whether this is the best motion the machine can make, whether a second person would call it the same, and whether anything is alive behind it.

## What was seen

**First replay, at durations sized for a 40°/s ceiling.** The author watched the seven segments and said:

> 1:少しだけまだある
> 2:ギュンの一歩手前

— the mid-motion wobble was still slightly there, and the launch was one step short of the stock's snap.

**What changed next was not the curve.** The stock firmware writes `WritePos(id, angle, 20, 0)`. This repository's firmware wrote `(0, 100)`. ⚠️ **Measured on the unit with one goal each way** — `bench timed 20 20 0`, then `bench timed -20 0 100` — the stock's call moved 112 counts (35°) in under 200 ms; ours moved the same 112 counts in about 1.0 s. **The 40°/s was a cap this repository was writing, not the servo's ceiling.**

**Second replay: the same seven segments, the same spring, the same durations, only the firmware's write changed.** 835 poses, 0 late. The author: still slightly there, still one step short.

**Third replay: the same seven segments at one-third the length.** 228 poses from the edge, 0 late. The board printed `410 applied, 0 refused, 0 clamped` — 228 poses plus 182 held. The author:

> かなり良い。これでOKだと思う。

The head ended at rest, and every segment landed six to eight counts short of its goal — 452 against 460, 517 against 524, 404 against 396 — the same fixed offset every trial shows, and not yet separated from backlash.

## What this does not say

⛔ **It does not say the motion is right; it says the author accepted it once, on this unit, today.** The spring's `sharpness = 5.0` is a copied number, not a measured one. The landing offset is unexplained. ⚠️ **And nothing here reads the head back**: the board prints, and no code in this repository listens.
