# The voice — 2026-10-18

⛔ **The observation is the spine of this file. The program's numbers are quoted inside it as evidence, not around it.** A log that holds only what the board printed cannot answer what this record is for — whether a word was audible — and a board that prints `done` reads as proof when it is not.

**What was counted.** One board, one evening, one pair of ears. **What was not counted:** loudness in decibels, the frequency each note actually left the speaker at, whether a second listener would have named the four words the same way, and anything the microphone on the same board would have heard — nothing here read it.

## What was heard

⚠️ **The fourth column of the wire had been silent since the contract was written**, and a field that is present but never played is not a channel. At **19:00 JST** — **before the 21:00 quiet-hours line**, so the hour was chosen on purpose — the four words were played one at a time, with the board answering each one, and the author was asked what reached them.

| # | what reached the author | what the board said at that moment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **all four words were heard** | `bench: playing` … `bench: done`, once per word |
| 2 | — | the same four words were then carried by frames, and the board printed the field |

⛔ **The answer is not a quotation and this file does not dress it as one.** The question was whether the four words were audible, and the answer was an affirmation of all four. ⚠️ **That is enough for one claim and for no other:** the speaker made a sound for each word. **It says nothing about whether any of them is pleasant, too loud, or the right tone for its transition** — those were not asked and not judged.

## What the wires carried

The console was opened the way the daemon opens it — `os.open` and raw `termios`, one open for the whole session, never `pyserial` — because the same board was wedged twice the same day by a reader that asserted DTR and RTS (records/2026-10-18-the-lights.md). **This session did not reset it and did not hang it.**

```
  5.40 bench: playing hello
  5.58 bench: hello done
  8.39 bench: playing pip
  8.41 bench: pip done
 11.61 bench: playing chime
 11.85 bench: chime done
 14.80 bench: playing fall
 15.11 bench: fall done
```

⚠️ **The gap between `playing` and `done` is not the length of the note, and it must not be read as one.** `i2s_channel_write` returns when the DMA buffers have taken the samples, not when the speaker has finished them: `pip` closes in 20 ms and `fall` in 310 ms, and none of those numbers is a duration this record can claim.

Then four frames, each carrying one word, and two refusals:

```
frame: light=warm face=happy gesture=look_up sound=pip
frame: light=dim face=sleepy gesture=none sound=chime
frame: light=dim face=none gesture=none sound=none
frame: refused (missing key)
frame: refused (unknown value)
```

- ⚠️ **The `chime` line printed four seconds after it was written**, because the frame before it carried `look_up` and the gesture motor blocks the console while the head moves. **A word behind a gesture is a word late** — measured here, not argued;
- ⛔ **the frame with no `sound` key was refused** (`missing key`), and the frame with `"sound":"horn"` was refused (`unknown value`). **The field is required and its words are closed** — the same rule the Python decoder asserts, spoken by the other end;
- ✅ **`"sound":null` was accepted** and played nothing. `null` means *this frame is silent*; it does not mean *keep the last one*.

## The amplifier, by the vendor's numbers

⛔ **Not one register value below was discovered on this unit.** They are the driver's (the esp_codec_dev AW88298 table the vendor's stack carries), read on this day, and the one number this repository changed is named:

| register | value | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `0x00` | `0x55AA` | software reset, then 50 ms |
| `0x04` | `0x4040` | I2S enabled, amplifier out of power-down |
| `0x05` | `0x0008` | SYSCTRL2, the vendor's init value |
| `0x06` | `0x3CC5` | Philips I2S, 16-bit frame, **rate code 5 = 24,000 Hz** |
| `0x0C` | `0x3064` | volume **−18 dB** — `0x30` is the field, `0x64` is another field left as the vendor leaves it |
| `0x61` | `0x0673` | the vendor's init value, purpose not read |

⚠️ **The init table's own `0x06` is `0x3CC8`**, whose low nibble is the sample-rate code for 48,000 Hz. **This board is driven at 24,000, and the code was replaced with `0x5` by hand** — a rate code and a sample rate that disagree is a silent, wrong-pitch channel, and nothing on this board would have reported it.

⚠️ **The reset line is not a register on the amplifier.** It is P0.2 on the AW9523 — the expander the display already owns — so `display::pulse_amplifier_reset()` holds that pulse and `sound` calls back into it. **One bus, one owner.**

## The four words

⛔ **These are choices, not measurements** — the same standing as the twelve LEDs' colours. No tone in this table was measured, matched to a reference, or judged against another. **The one that was kept is the one the ear accepted.**

| word | tones | what the shape is |
|---|---|---|
| `hello` | 659 Hz 90 ms → 880 Hz 150 ms | rises |
| `pip` | 1319 Hz 70 ms | one bright point |
| `chime` | 880 Hz 90 ms → 1319 Hz 200 ms | rises, and rings longer |
| `fall` | 659 Hz 110 ms → 440 Hz 260 ms | drops |

⚠️ **Which transition carries which word is a rule of the host**, not of this board: `hello` on `SessionStart`, `pip` on `PermissionRequest`, `chime` on `Stop`, `fall` on `StopFailure`, and `UserPromptSubmit` and `SessionEnd` are silent. ⛔ **The board knows the four words and nothing about when they are right** — it plays what the frame names.

⚠️ **The sound is off until the daemon's switch turns it on** (`--sound on|off`, default off) — the author's decision, so that installing this does not make a machine speak without being asked.

## What this record does not establish

- **It does not establish that the tones are good.** Four words were heard; **not one was judged against another**, and there is no reference tone in this workshop.
- **It does not establish the frequency or the loudness that left the speaker.** Nothing measured them; the ear said *a sound*, not *880 Hz*.
- **It does not establish that the engine ran.** No `Motion Intent` was read and no trajectory was computed. **The speaker answers words; it does not compute a motion.**
- **It does not establish that the Pet is there.** ⛔ **That is the goal, and it cannot be verified in this workshop.** A word that follows an event is a mechanism, not a presence.
