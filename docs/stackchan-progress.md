# The climb — a progress table toward a coding agent's body

⛔ **This page is a map, not a record.** Every line of it is derivable from a source elsewhere in the repository; when a source changes, this page is the one that is wrong. **Read it to find where the climb stands, never to learn what happened.**

**What is being built — and what is not.** ⛔ **The agent is not built here.** The agent is Claude Code, running in a session; **this repository builds the body it wears** — a face, a voice, twelve lights and a neck. ⛔ **No code here calls an LLM**, and the `Motion Intent` is written in the session, by Claude, guided by the skill. ⇒ **What can be checked here is the body's mechanism, not the agent's judgement.**

⚠️ **Two words, never mixed.** **The mechanism is verified** — trajectories, limits, protocols, deadlines. **The goal is not verified** — for it, only records of observation remain (`docs/verification-boundary.md`). ⇒ **A ✅ in this table says a mechanism runs. It says nothing about whether anything is there.**

## How to read a row

| mark | meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | built, and a named instrument measured it |
| ⚠️ | built, and not judged — the mechanism runs, and nobody has said it is right |
| ⛔ | not built |

⚠️ **Each row names what was counted and what was not.** **A row whose state moves depending on where you cut it does not belong in this table.**

## Where the climb stands

**Six of the eight are ✅, one is ⚠️, and one is ⛔ — measured 2026-10-19.** ⛔ **That is not "six-eighths of a coding agent":** the rows are questions, not weights, and the question below the table has no instrument at all.

**Rung 5 closed on 2026-10-19, and the instrument was an ear.** ✅ **What the ear did was compare one word against the others**, not a tone against anything: the author heard all four at the vendor's −18 dB and said 「少し小さい」; at `bench volume 90` — −10 dB — the author said that one of the four was small, and named it: **`fall`, the only word that ends on 440 Hz**, the lowest note of the set. ⇒ **The boot volume is now −10 dB and `fall`'s amplitude alone is 0.34 (the other three stay at 0.22)**, and the author heard it again and said 「OK」. ⚠️ **That is a judgement of loudness balance, and it is the judgement this row was waiting for** — ⛔ **the four shapes remain choices, and no measurement stands behind a single one of them.**

**Rung 7 is built, and it stopped at the machine.** ✅ **The code side is closed**: the daemon asks the board for the position at each gesture, on the fd it is about to stream on, and reads the answer under `PORT_WAIT = 0.5` — the bounded read this table asked for (`projects/stackchan/neck/readback.py`, ten tests). ✅ **Measured on the unit on 2026-10-19**: the query was written and the console answered — but the answer was `yaw=no answer pitch=no answer`, and `bench scan` found **0 id(s)**, before and after `bench power on`. ⛔ **Then the firmware side was asked the same question, and it answered earlier than the host could**: the boot log reads `servo: ping yaw=0/3 pitch=0/3` and `servo: a position did not read — nothing will move` — **the bus is silent before a single line is written by the host.** ✅ **And `bench power on` stopped being a print and became a question**: the command now checks the expander's answer, and a probe reads the six registers behind pin 0 — `M_L`/`O_L`/`PU_L` set, `DRV_L` = `0xFE` (push-pull), `I_L` = `0x00`. ⛔ **What `I_L = 0x00` means is not settled**: an expander's input register may report a pad only while the pin is an input, so it is consistent both with a line held low and with a register that is not reading the output. ✅ **Two more answers came the same day**: the PMIC reads a battery at **4,624 mV, charging**, and `bench level` reads pin 0's pad as LOW even when the output drives high. ⇒ **The supply exists at the battery; the rail's volts are still unmeasured** — the next thing a person can test is the power switch, and a meter closes the rest. ⇒ **The path is measured; the position is not, and no code here can produce it.** ✅ **Then the rail answered, and the row moved but did not close**: `bench power off`, three seconds, `bench power on` brought both ids back, and `bench state` returned the servos' own **5.1 V**, 31–32 °C and load 0 — **so the rail, the switch, the wiring and the expander are not what was wrong.** ⛔ **The silence is a state the servos hold across a reset of this chip**, and the rail is the only switch that clears it; since 0.19.31 boot throws it. ✅ **And one position came back**: `[28, 487]` at a `SessionStart`, and `look_up` streamed from it. ⛔ **The row stays ⚠️ because an answer is not the rung**: six of thirteen read-backs carried a position, the bus went silent again after a bounded move, and `heard: false` is now explained by the board's own source — `ConsoleTask` does not read while a gesture or a sound plays, so a line written then is merged or lost. ✅ **Since 0.19.33 the daemon keeps what the board said and asks once more**, and 8 of 8 probes were heard in a measured run — ⛔ **and frames are damaged the same way, which nothing on the host can see.** ⚠️ **A third silence was found that morning, in the log, with a window behind it: 1,368.7 s — about 22.8 minutes — in which no gesture was sent and nothing restarted**, and ⛔ **seven command-shaped attempts to force one all failed**; ⇒ **since 0.19.34 the firmware pings both ids every 5 s and throws the rail itself on three silences in a row**, for the measured 3 s. ✅ **Measured by taking the hands away: the console took `bench power off`, and the board was back in about 15 s** (`2026-10-19-the-machine-recovers-itself.md`). ⛔ **The row stays ⚠️: a silence is now noticed, cleared and printed, and that is not the same as one not happening.**

**Row 8 needs time, not code — but the one code-shaped part of it landed on 2026-10-19.** ⚠️ **Until 0.19.25 the only way to keep the night quiet was `--sound off`**, which keeps the day quiet too; ⇒ **the hour is now a rule** (`projects/stackchan/quiet.py`, thirty-three tests): 21:00–05:00 close the sound field and nothing else. ✅ **And at 05:27 the same morning the installed job was switched to `--sound on`** — the daytime now has a voice, and the night still does not. ⛔ **The row stays ⛔ because no day has been written down** — **the gate is open; the day does not exist.** ✅ **Since 0.19.28 that day can at least be counted**: `projects/stackchan/day/` reads the daemon's own log — seventeen tests, a pure core, an edge that opens one path — and its first reading found **3,852 lines, 11 starts, 3,599 decisions, 124 frames (every one `sent: true`), 48 streams, and 24 read-backs of which 0 carried a position**. ⛔ **A count is evidence for the record, not the record**: it does not say whether the body was right, wrong, or unnoticed — and ⛔ **not one restart in the log says why it happened.**

## What is not on this climb

⏸ **Machine 1, the DOF Reality box, is not a rung of this climb.** The owner parked it on 2026-10-19 — 「DOFは忘れて。もう、かなりしばらく繋がない」 — and **a coding agent's body does not need a three-axis rig.** The serial layer waits in `docs/construction-memo.md`, row 1, **and it waits without blocking anything here.**

⛔ **The agent itself is not on this climb either.** Its judgement, its memory and its choices are the session's; **this page measures only the body that session wears.**

## What this table cannot say

- ⛔ **It cannot say the body is good** — only that the mechanism computes what it was told to compute.
- ⛔ **It cannot say anything is there.** The goal's one record (`projects/stackchan-firmware/records/2026-10-19-something-is-there.md`) is one sentence, at N=1, and ⚠️ **the sentence had been offered a moment before.**
- ⛔ **It cannot be turned into a percentage**, and it must not be read as one.
- ⚠️ **It cannot say the eight questions are the right eight.** They are the ones the work has actually asked so far — **a ninth is added when something needs it, not before.**

**What was counted for this page:** the states of eight rows, the tests and records cited in them, and one source per row. **What was not counted:** anything in the room while the body is working — **that is row 8, and it is empty.**
